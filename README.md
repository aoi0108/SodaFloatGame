# 🍒 Soda Float Game

Welcome to **Soda Float Game**, a delightful and challenging arcade game that will put your timing to the test!

## 🍦 Simple & Addictive Gameplay
Cherries, both good and bad, are dropping from the sky. Your mission is simple: tap the screen at the perfect moment to land the good cherries right on top of the delicious soda float.

But be careful! You'll need to let the bad cherries fall to the ground.

## 🎮 How to Play

| Event | Action | Outcome |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Good Cherry** 🍒 | Land on float | **SCORE +1** |
| **Bad Cherry** 👾 | Let hit ground | **SCORE +1** |
| **Mistake** 😱 | Wrong sorting | **GAME OVER** |

## ✨ Features
* **One-Tap Control:** Easy to learn, but hard to master.
* **Test Your Reflexes:** A fun challenge for players of all ages.
* **Track Your Best:** Compete against yourself with the high score feature.
